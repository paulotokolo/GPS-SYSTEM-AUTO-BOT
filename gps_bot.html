Stop-Loss / Take-Profit ⚠️ Turning the stop-loss off means a trade has no automatic downside exit except your Daily Circuit Breaker or a manual close — real, uncapped risk on that trade. Leave it ON unless you specifically know why you want it off.
The stop is always fvg_swing_low — the middle candle of the 3-bar FVG. Take-profit off just means no fixed target; the trade then exits via Auto Trailing (if on) or the stop.
Why this exists: the levels here are absolute prices (e.g. SL 2001.80), and absolute prices are only proven to work on a modify request. Sending one on the opening order is unverified — if the platform rejects it, the whole order fails and no trade opens. After open sends a plain order first, then sets the levels immediately, so a bad SL can never cost you the entry. Switch to On open only if your broker requires it.
Optional pad below fvg_swing_low to survive spread/wick noise. 0 = exactly at fvg_swing_low, matching the indicator.
Auto Trailing ON: once a trade is far enough in profit, the stop-loss automatically moves up to lock in gains as price keeps going your way. OFF: the trade keeps its original GPS stop-loss and take-profit only, untouched.
Once a trade is up "Trail after profit" dollars, the stop starts following price, staying "Profit lock distance" dollars behind it. Tune both to your account size and instrument.
A raw breakeven stop at your exact entry still costs you the spread if it gets hit — a small guaranteed loss, not zero. This adds a cushion on top, so being stopped at breakeven means a tiny win or a true $0. Set it to roughly your spread + commission. Note: trailing only ever tightens the stop, never loosens it, so it can never move your GPS stop further from price than fvg_swing_low put it.
